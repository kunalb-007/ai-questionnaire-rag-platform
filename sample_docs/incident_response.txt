ACME CORPORATION — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document: IS-POL-005 — Incident Response Policy
Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: 01 March 2025 | Owner: CISO

================================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

This policy defines ACME Corporation's framework for identifying, containing,
eradicating, and recovering from information security incidents. It applies to
all systems, data, employees, contractors, and third-party service providers.

================================================================================
2. INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================

2.1 Severity Levels

Severity 1 — Critical
Definition: Active breach of customer data, ransomware deployment, or
compromise of production infrastructure. Executive escalation required.
Response time: Immediate (within 15 minutes of detection).

Severity 2 — High
Definition: Confirmed malware infection, unauthorised data access, or
compromise of an employee privileged account.
Response time: Within 1 hour of detection.

Severity 3 — Medium
Definition: Phishing email delivery, suspected account compromise, policy
violation with potential security impact.
Response time: Within 4 hours of detection.

Severity 4 — Low
Definition: Failed login attempts below lockout threshold, minor policy
violations, suspicious but unconfirmed activity.
Response time: Within 24 hours of detection.

================================================================================
3. INCIDENT RESPONSE PHASES
================================================================================

3.1 Preparation
The Security Operations Centre (SOC) maintains runbooks for the top 10 incident
types. All response team members complete tabletop exercises quarterly.

3.2 Identification
All employees are required to report suspected security incidents to the
Security Operations Centre via:
  - Email: security@acme.com
  - Phone: +1-800-ACME-SOC (24/7 hotline)
  - Internal portal: https://security.acme.internal/report

3.3 Containment
Short-term containment actions must begin within 30 minutes of Severity 1
classification. This may include isolating affected systems, blocking source
IP addresses, or revoking compromised credentials.

3.4 Eradication
Root cause must be identified and remediated before systems are returned to
production. All indicators of compromise (IOCs) are submitted to the threat
intelligence platform.

3.5 Recovery
Systems are restored from known-good backups or rebuilt from golden images.
A post-restoration monitoring period of 72 hours is required for Severity 1
incidents before full operational status is declared.

3.6 Lessons Learned
A post-incident review (PIR) must be completed within 5 business days of
incident closure for Severity 1 and 2 incidents, and within 10 business days
for Severity 3.

================================================================================
4. NOTIFICATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1 Internal Escalation
  - Severity 1: CISO and CEO must be notified within 30 minutes.
  - Severity 2: CISO must be notified within 1 hour.
  - Severity 3/4: Daily summary report to Information Security Manager.

4.2 Regulatory Notification
Data breaches affecting personal data of EU residents must be reported to the
relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours under GDPR Article 33.
Breaches affecting US residents in regulated sectors follow applicable state
breach notification laws (minimum: notify within 30 days for most US states).

4.3 Customer Notification
Customers whose data is confirmed to have been accessed or exfiltrated must
be notified within 72 hours of confirmation, regardless of regulatory
requirements.

================================================================================
5. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

Incident Commander (IC): Appointed per-incident from the SOC team. Owns
the incident from detection to closure.

Information Security Manager: Approves containment actions that affect
production services. Owns regulatory notification decisions.

CISO: Executive escalation point. Approves customer communications and
law enforcement engagement.

Legal Counsel: Consulted for all Severity 1 incidents and any incident
involving potential regulatory notification.

================================================================================
6. EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
================================================================================

All forensic evidence (logs, memory dumps, disk images) must be preserved
in write-protected storage before containment actions that might destroy
evidence. Chain of custody must be documented for any evidence that may
be used in legal proceedings.
